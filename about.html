UNITED MINING COMPANY

United Mining Company (UMC) is a Saudi industrial minerals company specialized in mining, mineral processing, industrial materials supply, engineering support, and industrial installation services. Established in 2009, UMC was formed to develop a focused industrial minerals platform capable of serving the growing requirements of Saudi Arabia’s construction, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, and industrial sectors.

The company operates across multiple sites within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including production and operating locations in the central, western, and eastern regions. UMC’s operational model combines mining activities, processing capabilities, logistics coordination, engineering support, and industrial execution to provide integrated solutions across the mineral value chain.

UMC’s operational background is built upon more than 65 years of accumulated experience in quarrying, aggregates, mining operations, and industrial materials supply through its ownership and management heritage. This operational experience supports the company’s practical approach toward production reliability, customer conformity, operational discipline, and scalable industrial growth.

The company produces and supplies a range of industrial mineral products including limestone, calcium carbonate, silica, quartz, talc, basalt, pozzolana, and barite for customers operating in construction, animal feed, cement, paints and coatings, plastics, glass manufacturing, drilling, industrial fillers, infrastructure, and other industrial applications.

UMC focuses on delivering products and services that conform to customer operational requirements, specifications, and application needs through controlled processing, practical quality assurance, operational coordination, and responsive execution.
