Our story

United Mining Company

Established in 2009, UMC was formed to develop a focused industrial minerals platform capable of serving the growing requirements of Saudi Arabia’s construction, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy and industrial sectors.

The company operates across multiple sites within the Kingdom, including production and operating locations in the central, western and eastern regions. UMC’s ownership and management heritage represents more than 65 years of accumulated experience in mining, quarrying and the supply of primary materials.
