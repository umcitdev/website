United Mining Company (UMC) is a Saudi industrial minerals company specialized in mining, mineral processing, industrial materials supply, engineering support, and industrial installation services.

Established in 2009, UMC was formed to develop a focused industrial minerals platform capable of serving the growing requirements of Saudi Arabia’s construction, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, and industrial sectors.
