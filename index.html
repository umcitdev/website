Who we are

A focused industrial minerals platform built for Saudi industry.

Established in 2009, UMC was formed to serve the growing requirements of Saudi Arabia’s construction, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, and industrial sectors. The company operates across multiple sites in the central, western, and eastern regions of the Kingdom.
